Overview
Skylar is an AI agent designed for Facebook advertising research, competitor intelligence, content optimization, and ad creative concept generation. This repository houses all prompt instructions and reference files needed to deploy Skylar without relying on external skill files.
Repository Contents
system_prompt.txt: The master prompt containing Skylar's persona, operational rules, workflow triggers, and output formatting standards.
brand_reference_guide.html: The minimalistic brand guide containing business identity, target audience profiles, service matrices, and ad concept templates.
reference_sources.md: Strategic resources, campaign structure guides, and market research frameworks.
README.md: Repository documentation and setup instructions.
How Skylar Operates
Skylar uses integrated workflows defined directly within the master prompt. The agent handles four main responsibilities:
Advertising Opportunity Research
Conducts market research, identifies audience pain points, and categorizes findings into strategic opportunity territories.
Competitor Advertising Analysis
Deconstructs competitor positioning, offers, creative formats, and angles to identify market gaps without copying competitor assets.
Content Improvement Briefs
Evaluates existing ad copy or creative assets and provides direct, prioritized edits and team handoff instructions.
Facebook Ad Concept Packs
Generates ready-to-test ad concepts featuring primary text, headlines, visual directions, calls to action, and single-variable test plans.
